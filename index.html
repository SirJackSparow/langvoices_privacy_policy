Privacy Policy for LangVoices

Effective Date: May 19, 2024
Last Updated: May 19, 2024

LangVoices ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by LangVoices.

This Privacy Policy applies to our mobile application (LangVoices). By downloading, accessing, or using LangVoices, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our application, including:

A. Account & Authentication Information

  • Email Address & Profile Data: When you create an account or sign in using Firebase Authentication or Google Sign-In, we collect your email address and basic profile identifiers to authenticate your account and sync your learning progress.

B. Camera & Scanned Content (Live Language Scanner)

  • Local Processing: Our Live Language Scanner feature uses your device's camera and Google ML Kit to recognize text or label objects in real time.
  • On-Device vs. Cloud:
    • Local AI: All image processing and text recognition occur entirely on your device. Images and text frames are not stored or sent to any external server.
    • Cloud AI: For this subscription feature, recognized text or object labels are transmitted securely to our AI backend solely to generate your language-learning lesson or translation.

C. Subscription & Billing Information

  • Google Play Billing: If you purchase a subscription for Cloud AI features, transactions are processed by Google Play. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers or financial details. We only receive confirmation of your active subscription status.

D. Analytics, Performance & Crash Reports

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: We collect usage statistics, app performance metrics, and crash logs to help identify bugs, optimize performance, and improve your learning experience.

E. Advertising Data

  • Google AdMob: Free-tier users may see advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, such as the Advertising ID, and interaction data to serve contextual or personalized advertisements in accordance with Google's advertising policies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain LangVoices, including vocabulary spaced-repetition reviews, quizzes, reading lessons, and AI conversation rooms.
  • Process and manage your account and subscription entitlements.
  • Improve app performance, troubleshoot technical issues, and analyze feature usage.
  • Serve advertisements to free-tier users.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted third-party service providers that help us operate our app:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: Authentication, secure database storage (Firestore), remote configuration, analytics, and crash reporting.
  • Google AdMob: Displaying advertisements to free-tier users.
  • Google Play Billing: Managing in-app purchases and subscriptions.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect your personal information against accidental loss, unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or storage system is completely secure.

5. Children's Privacy

LangVoices is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information from our servers.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of changes by posting the updated policy on this page and revising the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this policy periodically.
